# Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard

Analyzing workforce demographics, compensation, engagement, and employee turnover to identify retention opportunities.

![Dashboard Preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mohammed-Azhar-10/Employee-Attrition-and-Retention-Analysis/main/Attrition%20Ananlysis/Main%20Dashboard.jpeg)

---

## Project Overview

A company wants to understand employee attrition and workforce patterns to identify areas where HR can improve employee retention.

This Power BI project analyzes employee demographics, compensation, job satisfaction, workload, tenure, and other workforce factors to identify **high-attrition departments and employee segments**.

---

## Business Questions

* What does the company's workforce look like?
* Which departments and job roles have the highest attrition?
* How does attrition vary by age, tenure, salary, and gender?
* Is overtime associated with higher employee turnover?
* How do job satisfaction and work-life balance relate to attrition?
* Which employee segments should HR prioritize?

---

## Dataset

**HR Analytics Dataset — Kaggle**

* 1,470 employees
* Demographics
* Department and job role
* Monthly income
* Job satisfaction
* Work-life balance
* Overtime
* Business travel
* Years at company
* Attrition status

---

## Data Preparation

Data cleaning and transformation were performed using **Power Query**.

Key steps included:

* Data type correction
* Duplicate and missing-value checks
* Categorical standardization
* Age, income, tenure, and distance grouping
* Preparation of fields for analysis and segmentation

## Dashboard

The report contains three business-focused pages:

### 1. Workforce Overview

Provides a high-level view of employee count, active employees, attrition, average income, age, departments, roles, and workforce demographics.

## Key Findings

| Metric                       |                   Result |
| ---------------------------- | -----------------------: |
| Total Employees              |                    1,470 |
| Overall Attrition Rate       |                   16.12% |
| Highest Attrition Department |                    Sales |
| Sales Attrition Rate         |                   20.63% |
| Second Highest               | Human Resources — 19.05% |

Key patterns observed include higher attrition among certain **early-tenure, lower-income, overtime, and lower-satisfaction employee groups**.

These findings represent patterns in the dataset and are not treated as proof of causation.
